Is Levata right for you?

Honest about what we do, and what we don’t.

Levata is a hospitality and recovery retreat, not a medical facility. Surgical recovery guests should be able to manage their own medication and need only limited help getting in and out of bed. We do not provide wound care, clinical monitoring, or medication management.

A good fit if you
  • Can manage your own medications
  • Need only limited help with transfers
  • Want calm, private lodging instead of home or a hotel
Not the right fit if you need
  • Someone to manage your medications
  • Clinical monitoring or wound care
  • More than limited help moving around
