Is Levata right for you?

Honest about what we do, and what we don’t.

Levata is a hospitality and recovery retreat, not a medical facility. Knowing the difference up front helps you plan the right recovery.

A good fit if you
  • Can manage your own medications
  • Need only limited help getting in and out of bed
  • Want calm, private lodging instead of home or a hotel
Not the right fit if you need
  • Someone to manage your medications
  • Clinical monitoring or wound care
  • More than limited help moving around

Questions

Good to know.

Can I come straight from surgery?

Yes. Early check-in from noon is available based on occupancy.

Do I have to use a specific surgeon?

No. We welcome guests from any practice.

What happens in an emergency?

Staff are on site 24 hours a day and will call 911 and your emergency contact.

Can someone stay with me?

Yes. A companion can stay in your suite for $75 a night, including meals.
