Questions

For hosts.

Do I need to be affiliated with Levata?

No. Independent professionals may apply. Approved events are clearly labeled with the host or organizer.

Who handles registration and payment?

Hosts use their own registration platform. Levata links families directly to the confirmed registration page.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Update your registration page and contact Levata promptly so the public calendar can be updated.

Can I sell products at my class?

Product sales and sampling are reviewed as part of each host application.
