Community events

Our doors are open to the community.

You don’t need to stay with us to be part of Levata. Throughout the year, local professionals teach classes and host gatherings in our space, open to anyone who wants to learn, connect, or simply get out of the house for a while.

What we host

Classes for everyone.

Whether you’re expecting, home with a new baby, supporting someone you love, or simply looking for community, there’s a place for you here. Classes are led by professionals who teach in our space, so topics vary over time.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be a Levata guest?

No. Events are open to everyone in the community.

How much do classes cost?

Each host sets their own price. Some events are free.

Can I bring my baby?

Most gatherings welcome babies. Check each event listing for details.

Where do I go?

Levata Retreat, 1133 W 3200 N, Lehi, UT 84043. Free parking is available.
