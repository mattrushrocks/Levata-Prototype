Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be a Levata guest?

No. Events are open to the community unless a listing says otherwise.

How much do classes cost?

Select activities may be complimentary for overnight guests. Independent presenters set their own registration prices.

Who do I contact about an event?

Use the registration link on the event listing to reach the organizer directly.

Can I bring my baby?

Guest eligibility varies by event. Check each confirmed listing for details before registering.

Where do I go?

Levata Retreat, 1133 W 3200 N, Lehi, UT 84043. Free parking is available.
