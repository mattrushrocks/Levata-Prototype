About Levata

Boilerplate

Levata is a hospitality and recovery retreat in Lehi, Utah, created for people who want a private suite, thoughtful meals, and practical support while they recover. Postpartum stays include nursery access and basic lactation guidance; surgical recovery stays provide non-medical lodging and limited everyday assistance while each guest’s physician remains in charge of care.

Fast facts

  • Located in Lehi, Utah
  • Opening in 2027
  • Private studio and one-bedroom suites
  • Postpartum and surgical recovery stays
  • Three-night minimum
  • Studio $700; one-bedroom $850 per night
  • Community classes and gatherings
