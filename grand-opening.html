Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be planning a stay?

No. Family, friends, providers, and curious neighbors are welcome to RSVP.

Can I bring my family?

Yes. Include everyone in your RSVP so we can plan accordingly.

Will you share what a stay costs?

Yes. Pricing is published on the website and our concierge will be available to answer questions.

Is Levata a medical facility?

No. Levata is a hospitality and recovery retreat. Guests remain under the care of their own physician or surgeon.
